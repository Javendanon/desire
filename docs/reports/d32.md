# D32: Trollskull Manor floor plan as data + 5 ft square grid

Status: ready for review. This card is implemented by the owner's Claude session, not by a Flow agent.

## What changed

- **Contract** (`contracts/schema.json`, new `interior` module): adds `FloorPlan`, `Floor`, `Room`,
  `CellRect`, `CellEdge`, `Door`, `EdgeMark`, `Furniture`, `FloorLink` (stairs, ladder, trapdoor,
  slide), `Point3`, `PropCatalog`, `Prop`, `PropVariant` and their enums. The generated files are
  `crates/core/src/contracts/interior.rs` and `src/generated/contracts.ts`.
- **Data:**
  - `content/maps/waterdeep/trollskull/floor-plan.json` covers six floors: sótano, planta baja, primer
    piso, segundo piso, buhardillas and cúspide de la torreta.
  - It holds 30 rooms, 67 pieces of furniture and 7 links. One of the links is the giant one-way
    slide from the top of the turret to the alley, with its 41-point spiral path.
  - `content/props/catalog.json` has 24 kinds with colour and material variants.
  - The plan images are not committed (public repository). The source and the reading choices are
    in `content/maps/waterdeep/trollskull/README.md`.
- **Engine** (pure, no GPU):
  - `math/square.ts`: square cells and canonical edges.
  - `plan/floor-index.ts`: per-floor lookups.
  - `plan/floor-board.ts`: movement, with diagonals counting as one move, walls, doors, blocking
    furniture and links.
  - `plan/floor-edges.ts`: the walls, doors and windows the model draws.
  - `plan/floor-validation.ts`: readable problems by id.
  - The hex board stays for outdoor scenes.

## Advisor

An independent review found no blocking issues. All of its findings are addressed:

- a test for the second half of the diagonal rule and a closed door on a corner;
- links are validated against the real catalogue, so they cannot land on furniture;
- doors and windows must sit in a wall, and openings must join two rooms;
- one mark per edge;
- links to a missing floor are reported;
- furniture cannot seal a door;
- the rotation convention and the wall-finish choice are documented.

## Limits

- The transcription is a reading of a hand-drawn plan, so some squares may be off by one. The DM
  editor (D36) will allow corrections per campaign without touching this file.
- Elevation order of links is not validated, because links may go up or down.
- There is no spawn rule yet. The interior scene (D34) decides where miniatures start.
