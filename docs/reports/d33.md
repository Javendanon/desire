# D33: Trollskull Manor model (Blender headless, one GLB per floor and per piece)

Status: ready for review. This card is implemented by the owner's Claude session (`sesion-claude`).
It is based on D32 (PR #36).

## What changed

- **Geometry** (`tools/interior/src/`, TypeScript): turns the FloorPlan into boxes and cylinders in
  glTF axes:
  - a slab per room rectangle;
  - walls on the edges D32 derives;
  - a lintel over each door, and a sill, lintel and glass pane for each window;
  - steps, ladders and the cellar hatch for the links;
  - the slide's path, with posts every few points.
- **Furniture** is declared as data in `tools/interior/prop-parts.json`: 24 kinds, each part with a
  material. The `variant` part is tinted at runtime from the catalogue.
- **Build** (`tools/interior/build.py`, Blender 5.2 headless): merges the shapes into one mesh per
  category per floor and sweeps a U-shaped trough along the slide path. It exports:
  - six floor GLBs;
  - `tobogan.glb`;
  - 24 furniture GLBs.

  It saves `.local/interior/trollskull.blend` (not in git). Objects under `Manual/<floor id>`
  survive rebuilds. `derive.ts` refuses to write when the plan does not validate.
- **Docs:**
  - `docs/asset-pipeline.md` records the owner's exception to the "no GLB" decision for
    hand-authored interiors;
  - `docs/design/art-provenance.md` lists the models as original;
  - `tools/interior/README.md` explains rebuilds and manual edits.

## Advisor

The independent review found one blocking bug: every cylinder was wound inside-out, so posts,
round tables, stools and barrels would have been culled. It is fixed. With the old winding, a
rebuilt round table has a side normal of −0.98 on its +x side, which the new normals test rejects.

Also addressed:
- the drift check is now a sha256 manifest of the whole build input;
- the cell size comes from the plan;
- a material without a Principled node gets one instead of crashing;
- objects also linked under `Manual` are never deleted;
- hidden manual objects are reported;
- the unused yaw path was removed;
- the missing coverage was added.

Steep stairs are documented as a known limit.

## Limits

- The turret is square. Octagonal chamfers, roofs and baked ambient occlusion come later.
- Stairs fit in one square, so they are steep (about 63°).
