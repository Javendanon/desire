## Validation rule

`load_entries` requires, for every entry, an `edition_verification` object with a
known status; a `verified` entry also needs a non-empty citation. `check_verification`
then requires the entry's registered source to have both `document_url` (http/https)
and `document_sha256` (64 lowercase hex), and the citation to contain that digest.
A 2024 entry without the block therefore fails in `load_entries`, `importer.sync` and
`importer --check`, with `spells.json[1].edition_verification: expected object`.

A self-referential citation (just the source locator) is rejected, as is a citation
carrying a digest from another document. The importer stays offline: it can enforce
that the claim points at a registered, hashed document, but cannot fetch the document
or prove that the cited section exists. That remains a reviewer step.
