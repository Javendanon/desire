## Coverage and limits

- The visual cross-check used the Jason Engle raster downloaded from AideDD; its
  SHA-256 matched the recorded snapshot (`45f5f862...0e16`). Crops were generated
  with `ffmpeg` into `/tmp`, not committed, because they reproduce the artwork.
- The seven newly enumerated points were inspected on 700 x 700 crops with a
  centered red cross. The gates landed on their gatehouses, Deepwater Beacon on the
  southern beacon structure, and New Olamn on the Cliffride building the dataset
  names "New Olamn's academy of music and other arts".
- Streets remain label anchors. The AideDD dataset stores one point per street and
  no geometry, so no street graph is claimed.
- The scale is still source-asserted and not independently georeferenced. Nothing
  here changes that.
- `km` magic-number hits in the verifier are the named `EXPECTED` constants;
  documented as false positives.
- Rust tests remain blocked by the pre-existing missing Axum dependency (D00); not
  touched by this card.
