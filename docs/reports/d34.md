# D34: playable Trollskull Manor in the engine

Status: ready for review. This card is implemented by the owner's Claude session (`sesion-claude`).
It is stacked on D33 (#37) and D32 (#36).

## What changed

- **`InteriorViewer`** (`src/lib/engine/interior-viewer.ts`) loads the D33 GLBs through PlayCanvas
  containers. `boot.ts` gains a `loadsModels` option that registers the texture and container
  loaders. The viewer can:
  - place and tint every piece of furniture from the catalogue (one shared tinted material per
    colour);
  - show the active floor and the floors under it;
  - put miniatures on the 5 ft squares;
  - light the squares a miniature can reach;
  - walk it square by square;
  - ride the slide along its spiral at an even speed;
  - fly a 0..1 camera path.
- **Pure modules with tests:**
  - `plan/interior-placement.ts`: square centres with floor origins, poses of turned furniture,
    picking, slide sampling by arc length;
  - `plan/interior-camera.ts`;
  - `math/camera-path.ts`.
- **`lifecycle.ts`:** once-only teardown with a live set. It removes the destroy preamble the three
  viewers had copied (the gate's rule of three).
- **`interior.html` + `src/design/interior-demo.ts`:** a development page with floor and miniature
  buttons and click-to-move.
- **`tests/browser/interior-smoke.mjs`** is added to the gate suites. It checks:
  - the house loads on WebGL 2;
  - the floor switch;
  - the kitchen wall blocks and the pantry door lets a miniature through;
  - the slide takes a miniature from the turret to the alley;
  - destroy stops the loop and leaves no live viewer.

## Advisor

An independent review found nothing blocking. Addressed:

- assets that finish loading after `destroy()` are released;
- a miniature still walking refuses a new move instead of jumping;
- free movement outside move mode is documented as the DM's path;
- pieces under 1 m cast no shadow;
- the step-or-slide decision is a pure, tested `routeLegs` (a slide is ridden only downwards);
- the empty camera path is explained in a comment.

## Limits

- About 460 draw calls on the ground floor at low quality (504 before short pieces stopped casting
  shadows). Furniture is not batched yet.
- The colours lean warm and saturated at low quality (no SSAO). The design pass comes with the
  prologue work.
- The models still have a square turret, no roofs and steep stairs (D33 limits).
