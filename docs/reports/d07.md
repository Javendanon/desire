## Delivered

- `content/knowledge/`: licensed source registry, eight canonical Spanish entries
  (spells, classes, conditions) with 2014/2024 editions, translation status,
  provenance, license and references.
- `content/knowledge/compendium.json`: deterministic D04 `KnowledgeEntry` projection
  plus a D07 metadata sidecar.
- `content/knowledge/campaign/demo-campaign.json`: synthetic demo lore and a DM secret
  with explicit visibility, kept outside canon.
- `tools/knowledge/`: offline canon loader, campaign validator, D04 adapter,
  idempotent importer and Spanish search/filter CLI.
- `tools/knowledge/tests/`: 82 tests for import, validation, field/shape guards,
  licensing, references, campaign separation, visibility, offline operation, CLI
  gating and search.

## Scope decisions

The seed uses only SRD 5.1 and SRD 5.2 under CC-BY-4.0. The registry does not assume
that other D&D material is open. Bodies are short interpretive Spanish translations,
not verbatim SRD text; `translation_status` is `translated`, not `reviewed`, and D04
`evidence` is `interpretation`. Where 2014 and 2024 mechanics are identical, the
Spanish body is intentionally identical and the edition remains explicit in the entry.

D04 `KnowledgeEntry` has no kind, translation-status or references fields. D07 keeps
those fields in its own catalog sidecar and projects only the exact D04 fields into
`knowledge_entries`, so a future persistence consumer can store the contract DTO
without unknown fields. If the shared contract later needs these fields, that is a D04
contract change, not a D07 edit.

The generated artifact carries `source_id`, `license` and `provenance`; the source
registry keeps the license URL and source URL. Consumers that need those URLs join on
`source_id` instead of duplicating them in every entry.

## Limitations

- The seed is intentionally small; it is not a complete spell/class/condition catalog.
- D04 schema validation covers the projected DTO shape, not persistence, authorization,
  relational uniqueness or API behavior.
- The D02 gate does not measure `tools/knowledge`; the new scope was measured manually
  with `km`, and its tests are a separate command.
- No network, Docker or external service was used.
